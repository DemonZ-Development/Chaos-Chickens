# 🐔 Chaos Chickens Wiki

Welcome to the official wiki of **Chaos Chickens**! This wiki is your comprehensive guide to configuring, custom-developing, and enjoying everything the mod/plugin has to offer.

---

> [!NOTE]
> **Chaos Chickens** is a premium, high-performance, and multi-platform (Bukkit/Paper/Folia, Fabric, and Forge) Minecraft mod/plugin that injects unpredictable, action-packed fun into your Minecraft worlds by infusing regular chickens with spectacular, custom traits.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

* **11+ Custom Traits**: From standard speed boosts to explosive deaths, magnet fields, ore generator layers, disco particles, zombie mutations, and cursed potions.
* **👑 Boss Chickens**: Extremely rare and formidable chickens boasting **multiple active traits combined**, boosted health, and special player-debilitating weakness auras!
* **Multi-Platform Support**: Built from the ground up using a shared engine, meaning Bukkit/Paper, Fabric, and Forge versions operate with identical traits and logic.
* **⚡ High Performance & Folia Ready**: Employs region-aware asynchronous updates on Paper/Folia and highly optimized caching on Forge/Fabric, ensuring zero tick lag.
* **📊 Developer API**: Register your own custom traits and behaviors using a clean, synchronized, and highly documented API.
