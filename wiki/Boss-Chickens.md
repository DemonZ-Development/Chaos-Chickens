# 👑 Boss Chickens Guide

**Boss Chickens** are the ultimate challenge in **Chaos Chickens**. They represent the apex of randomized chaos and will test even fully-geared players.

---

## 👹 Boss Specifications

* **Appearance**: Spawns with a bold dark red custom tag: `BOSS Chicken` and a dynamic **Dragon Breath particle aura** billowing around them.
* **Health Pool**: Has a massive **12.0 HP** (3x the default chicken health of 4.0 HP).
* **Weakness Aura**: Emits an oppressive aura that inflicts `WEAKNESS I` on any player within a **16-block radius** (refreshed every 2 seconds). This drastically decreases your melee damage, making it extremely difficult to defeat them quickly with swords!
* **Ultimate Death explosion**: When slain, they trigger a massive, safe visual explosion accompanied by an expanding cloud of 30+ Dragon Breath particles.

---

## ⚡ Multi-Trait Combination

What makes a Boss Chicken truly terrifying is that **they inherit multiple chaos traits simultaneously**! When a Boss Chicken spawns, it rolls the global trait pool up to 3 times (configurable via `boss-trait-count` / `bossTraitCount`) and combines their effects:

* **Example combination: Speed + Explosive + Cursed**
  * You face a lightning-fast chicken that you cannot easily hit.
  * If you get close, you are blinded and poisoned by the curse.
  * When you finally kill it, it detonates right in your face!
* **Example combination: Golden + Teleport + Ice**
  * A glowing golden chicken that creates frozen ice walkways.
  * Every time you attempt to hit it to get the diamonds/emeralds, it teleports 10 blocks away!

---

## ⚔️ Combat Strategies

1. **Ranged Attacks**: Since the Weakness Aura completely ruins your sword damage within 16 blocks, using a **Bow or Crossbow** is highly recommended to take down a Boss Chicken from a safe distance.
2. **Water Buckets**: A water bucket can be useful to slow down speed-boosted boss chickens, although watch out if they have the **Ice** trait, as they will freeze your water source!
3. **Piston Traps**: Using pistons or leads to trap the boss chicken allows you to easily harvest them or keep them as high-value decorations/defenders.
